Rx Organizer

Privacy Policy · Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version

Rx Organizer keeps your family's health records on your own phone. There is no account to create, no server holding your family history, and nothing is uploaded in the background. We collect no analytics and receive no usage reports. Nobody, including the people who built the app, can see what you store.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — profiles, ages, conditions, medicines, doctor visits, and the photos or PDFs of prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries, ID and insurance cards — is saved in the app's own storage on your device. None of it is transmitted anywhere unless you turn on AI reading and ask for a specific document to be read.

With AI reading turned off

Nothing leaves your phone. Not a photo, not a name, not a date. The app works fully offline in this state.

With AI reading turned on

AI reading is optional and off by default. When it is on, only the one document you tap Read on is sent to be read, and only at that moment. It is sent to Google's Gemini AI service.

  • Using the app owner's AI. The document passes through a small relay server the app owner operates on the way to Google. That server keeps no copy of the document and does not log its contents. The Google account behind it is a paid one, which means Google does not use your documents to train its models.
  • Owner-configured AI only. Regular users cannot enter their own AI key. Only the app owner can configure which AI provider is used, from a screen unlocked with an admin key.

Requesting AI access

To use the app owner's AI you enter an invite code. A name you choose for yourself is then shown on the owner's management screen, alongside a randomly generated number that identifies your installation. That is the entirety of what the owner can see. They cannot see your records, and they can switch an individual installation's access off without affecting anything else on your phone.

Backups

A backup is a file you create yourself, from Settings. Where it goes — your phone's storage, Google Drive, Gmail, a chat app — is entirely your choice. The app keeps no copy of it and has no way to reach it afterwards. Anyone holding that file holds your records, so store it somewhere private.

Sharing and selling

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, insurers, employers, data brokers, or anyone else. There is no advertising in the app and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from Android Settings, permanently removes every record. There is nothing held anywhere else that needs deleting, and no request you need to send us. If you had AI access through the app owner, you may ask them to remove your installation from their list using the contact address below.

Children

The app is designed for an adult keeping records for their own family, children included. It is not directed at children and is not intended to be used by children on their own.

Not medical advice

Rx Organizer files your documents and reads them back to you. It does not diagnose, does not make decisions, and is not a substitute for a doctor. AI reading can misread handwriting, skip a line, or place a number wrongly. Always check what the app shows against the original document, never change a medicine because of what you read here, and in an emergency contact a doctor or go to hospital.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the copy inside the app is updated in the same release.
